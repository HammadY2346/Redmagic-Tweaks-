The first public release of RedMagic Tweaks.

**Tested on:** REDMAGIC 10S Pro / 10 Pro (NX789J), China firmware RedMagic OS 11.0.11 (Android 16). Global and EEA
firmware differ a little and are not tested yet; reports are welcome.

### What's in it
- **One UI 8 look** in one tap: Control Center with pills, labelled round tiles and vertical sliders, One UI lock
  screen clock, notification cards, volume panel, power menu, status bar icons and the Inter System UI font. Back to
  stock in one tap.
- **279 tweaks in 13 categories**: status bar, quick panel, notifications, lock screen & AOD, fingerprint, volume &
  power, charging & battery, display & performance (auto refresh rate up to 165 Hz), home, drawer & recents, gaming,
  fonts, themes & boot, system & apps, plus Advanced. Each category card lists what is inside it.
- **One UI 8 volume panel and power menu, your way**: panel width and height, bar shape, colours and gradients,
  bar background, speaker icon inside or under the bar, a level number; power-menu button size, shape, colours and
  label style.
- **Recent apps styles** for the stock launcher: One UI 8, carousel, stack (iOS style), cover flow (3D), grid.
- **Previews everywhere**: every look and most options are drawn before you apply them.
- **Restarts that really apply**: one bar collects what is waiting and restarts System UI or the changed apps, then
  checks that System UI really restarted.
- **Detailed mode**: shows what every setting does right under its name (menu › Detailed mode).
- **Fail-safe**: Safe mode switch and Quick Settings tile, crash guard, boot-loop guard, reset all (with a backup), and
  `su -c setprop persist.redmagictweaks.safe 1` from outside the app.
- **Saved layouts**: save your setup, switch between setups, export and import them.

### Install
1. Install the APK below.
2. In LSPosed, enable RedMagic Tweaks and tick every app in its scope.
3. Reboot once, then open the app.
